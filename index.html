Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you're looking for? Use the contact form below.

What areas in Rwanda do you serve?
We operate across Kigali and all major cities in Rwanda, including Butare, Rwamagana, Musanze, and Huye. For locations outside Kigali, we assess on a case-by-case basis to ensure we maintain our service standards.
How do I get a quote?
You can reach us through the contact form on this page, call us directly, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll discuss your space, understand your needs, and provide a tailored quotation within 24 hours.
Do you offer one-time or recurring services?
Both. We accommodate one-time deep cleaning or event support, and we also offer recurring maintenance contracts — daily, weekly, monthly, or as your schedule requires.
What types of properties do you manage?
We work with offices, retail spaces, hotels, schools, hospitals, warehouses, residential apartments, and private homes. Our integrated model means we adapt to the specific needs of each property type.
Are your cleaning products eco-friendly?
We prioritize environmentally responsible products where possible and are transitioning to greener supplies across all divisions. We can also advise clients on sustainable product choices for their properties.
